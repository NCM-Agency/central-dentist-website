Dr. Jill Ombrello recently joined Holistic Hilda on her podcast to answer a question many parents ask: why do healthy kids still get cavities, even when they brush and try to eat well?

In the conversation, Dr. Jill shares a biological dentistry view of how cavities actually form, and why the full picture goes beyond sugar and brushing. She and Hilda talk through the role of nutrition, the balance of the mouth's natural bacteria, and how a child's growth and airway connect to their dental health.
